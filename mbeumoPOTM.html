OCTOBER'S PLAYER OF THE
MONTH ANNOUNCED

by Adam Marshall
Friday 31 October 2025 19:19

Bryan Mbeumo has been voted our Player of the Month for the second time in his short Manchester United career.

The brilliant forward picked up the trophy for his exploits in August, making an immediate impact after arriving from Brentford in the summer.

After Bruno Fernandes took the honours for the Tiger-sponsored accolade last month, Bryan's contribution to three successive wins proved enough to beat off competition from Senne Lammens, Matthijs de Ligt and Matheus Cunha for the prize.

Mbeumo secured 67 per cent of the vote, finishing well clear of Lammens (21%), De Ligt (8%) and Cunha (4%).

The Cameroon international supplied the assist for Mason Mount's excellent opener in the 2-0 win over Sunderland and took just over a minute to fire United ahead at Anfield, in the memorable 2-1 victory against Liverpool.

Last time out, he struck twice as Brighton & Hove Albion were beaten 4-2 at Old Trafford, to also help clinch a place on the shortlist for the Premier League's overall individual award for October.

Meanwhile, Harry Maguire's winner at Liverpool won our Goal of the Month award, in the face of some stiff competition.

The centre-back's header, from Bruno Fernandes's volleyed cross, came out on top of our fan poll.

Congratulations to both Bryan and Harry!
