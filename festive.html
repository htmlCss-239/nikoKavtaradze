SEVEN HOME FESTIVE WINS
TO REMEMBER

by Adam Marshall
Saturday 01 November 2025 22:05

Manchester United have had some special occasions at Old Trafford over the festive period in the past, and let's hope this will be the case again in 2025.

Our fixtures have now been confirmed and there is also a chance to have your own night to remember at the Theatre of Dreams with Christmas party bookings available.

Dinner, drinks and entertainment packages are being sold across three evenings in early December.

Find out more below, but in the meantime, check out these fabulous wins on the pitch that provided cheer for the United faithful...

United 3 Aston Villa 2 (26/12/2023)

Rasmus Hojlund and Alejandro Garnacho inspire a second-half comeback on Boxing Day, after going two goals down before the break courtesy of John McGinn and Leander Dendoncker.

United 3 Nottingham Forest 0 (27/12/2022)

Goals from Marcus Rashford, Anthony Martial and Fred ensure victory - it was the first time we'd played Forest in the league since the record 8-1 away win in 1999.

United 4 Bournemouth 1 (30/12/2018)

Goals from Paul Pogba (2), Rashford and Romelu Lukaku account for the Cherries as 2018 draws to a close.

United 4 Newcastle 3 (26/12/2012)

Jonny Evans, Patrice Evra and Robin van Persie net in a Boxing Day classic after Newcastle lead 3-2 in a topsy-turvy encounter. Javier Hernandez's late winner helps earn three valuable points en route to our most recent Premier League title.

United 5 Wigan Athletic 0 (26/12/2011)

Wayne Rooney, Michael Carrick, Rafael, Dimitar Berbatov and Antonio Valencia, against his former club, all score in a one-sided victory.

United 3 Forest 0 (26/12/1998)

Ronny Johnsen is an unlikely two-goal hero, and Ryan Giggs is also on the mark, with Forest downed during the Treble-winning campaign.

United 5 Coventry City 0 (28/12/1992)

In the first year of the Premier League, Giggs, Mark Hughes, Eric Cantona - from the penalty spot - Lee Sharpe and Denis Irwin all score as the Sky Blues are put to the sword.
